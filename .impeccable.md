## Design Context

### Users
Developers and ML learners who can write `A @ x` but have no mental picture of it. Usually studying alongside 3Blue1Brown or Strang, at a desk, often in the evening. The job: build a matrix, watch space transform, and connect the picture to the math and the NumPy code.

### Brand Personality
Precise, tactile, inked. An engineering notebook: careful hand-drawn diagrams on graph paper, annotations in the margin. It should feel like studying with a good notebook, not using a dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Light theme: cream engineering paper with a fine blue graph grid, blue-black ink, red-pencil accents.
- Dark theme: a black notebook (near-black warm paper, not blueprint blue), white gel-pen ink, the same pencil accents.
- Theme follows the system by default, with a persistent manual toggle.
- Basis colors keep the 3Blue1Brown convention (î green, ĵ red), tuned per theme for contrast.
- Type: Bricolage Grotesque (display), Public Sans (UI and reading), JetBrains Mono (code and numbers), Architects Daughter (hand-lettered canvas annotations only).
- Anti-references: generic SaaS dashboards (cards, pills, glows everywhere), neon cyberpunk / green-on-black "Matrix" terminal.

### Design Principles
1. The plot is the hero; chrome stays quiet and paper-like.
2. Ink, not glow: solid strokes, hatching instead of translucent fills, no gradients or shadows for decoration.
3. Annotate like a person would: hand lettering only where a human would write on the diagram.
4. Color carries meaning (î, ĵ, each vector, eigen lines), so the UI itself stays nearly monochrome.
5. Both themes are first-class and pass WCAG AA for text.
